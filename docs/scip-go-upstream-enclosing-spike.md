## Why the downstream strict IR count did not rise

SCIP now has 58 candidates with a valid full declaration range, but the v1.3 syntax Code IR has **no units for 20 Go struct fields**. Its 11 same-name type-unit candidates begin **after** the Go `type ` keyword while the SCIP declaration correctly includes it; ten differences are exactly `type ` and one also includes the type's attached documentation. As a result, 58 SCIP symbols divide into **27 exact syntax unit joins, 11 declaration-boundary mismatches and 20 missing field units**. The 117 normalized local SCIP references are real additional semantic candidates, but 99 must still abstain from a bound `object_id` under the existing exact-body/kind contract. The separately measured same-source gopls LSP path bound 73/117 of its own reference set. These denominators and source definitions differ; do not claim a precision regression or rank the producers from these numbers alone.

**Reassessment:** The upstream change is valuable **independently of zvec**: it expands standard SCIP call-hierarchy/declaration evidence with no primary navigation-site churn, passes upstream tests, and makes positions self-describing. It is reasonable to propose upstream as a focused follow-up to #92, ideally keeping explicit Go encoding as its own small change for review. In the **historical v1.3** snapshot above, it did **not** complete the strict IR integration by itself. The now-committed `spike/code-ir-design` v1.5 frontend added canonical Go type ranges and source-backed struct-field `value` units; its read-only recheck strictly joins all **117 normalized patched local candidates** ([current result](scip-go-post-code-ir-v15-revalidation.md)). Full independent relation precision, negative-site corpus, dependency-complete freshness and ranking gates remain open. No production ranking/default change follows from either successful producer/IR spike.
